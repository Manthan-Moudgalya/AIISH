# AIISH Tele-consultation Tool

This project is developed for **AIISH (All India Institute of Speech and Hearing)** to facilitate remote consultations and therapy management for clients needing speech, language, and audiological support. The platform provides an end-to-end digital experience for patients and clinicians, including appointment scheduling, therapy tracking, and interactive communication tools.

## 🌐 Project Overview

The AIISH Tele-consultation Tool is a secure and user-friendly web-based platform designed to assist **SLPs (Speech-Language Pathologists)** and **Audiologists** in delivering therapy and consultations remotely. It enables seamless communication, structured therapy sessions, and efficient record-keeping, ensuring continuity of care and personalized client support.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 🗓️ Appointment Scheduling

* Easy-to-use interface for clients to schedule appointments with SLPs or Audiologists.
* Calendar view with available slots and automated confirmation system.

### 2. 💬 AI Chatbot & Messaging System

* Integrated chatbot to answer FAQs and guide users.
* Live messaging feature for real-time support between clients and clinicians.

### 3. 🔔 Automated Reminders

* Sends automated reminders 3 days, 1 day, and on the day of the appointment.
* Helps reduce no-shows and ensures clients are prepared.

### 4. 📁 Medical Records Management

* Securely stores client medical records and therapy notes for up to 12 months.
* Enables clinicians to access and update records quickly.

### 5. 📊 Therapy Progress Tracking

* Tools to log and track client’s progress before and after therapy.
* Visual dashboards for both clinicians and clients to monitor improvements.

### 6. 📝 Shared Notes

* Real-time collaborative space for clinicians and clients.
* Enables shared annotations, instructions, and visual therapy plans.

### 7. 🖥️ Screen Sharing

* Allows clinicians to share videos, presentations, and educational resources.
* Enhances the clarity and effectiveness of remote sessions.

### 8. 🎮 Customizable Therapy Activities

* Upload, manage, and assign custom therapy exercises, games, and tasks.
* Helps tailor treatment plans to individual client needs.

### 9. 🎥 Session Recording & Playback

* Option to record sessions for review and documentation.
* Clients and clinicians can replay sessions for follow-up and reinforcement.
